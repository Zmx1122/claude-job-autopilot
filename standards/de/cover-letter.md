# Germany · Cover letter standard (Anschreiben / Cover Letter)

Apply these rules to every cover letter for the German market unless the user says otherwise.

## Format
1. One page, **300–450 words, 4 paragraphs**. Language = the posting's language.
2. Same header as the CV; date; recipient = full company name + department or contact person.
3. **Subject line**: `Bewerbung als <Stellentitel> – Kennziffer <Nr.>` / `Application for <job title> – Ref. <no.>`
   (omit the reference part when the posting has none).
4. **Salutation**: contact person named in the posting → `Sehr geehrte Frau X` / `Dear Ms X`; otherwise
   `Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren` / `Dear Hiring Team`.

## Structure
5. **Paragraph 1 — why this company and this role**: one specific, verifiable fact about the company (product, business,
   team direction) taken from a page you actually opened; then one sentence with the candidate's core value.
   Never open with "Hiermit bewerbe ich mich …" / "I am writing to apply …".
6. **Paragraph 2 — the most relevant experience**: answers the posting's top 2–3 requirements with concrete technology,
   the candidate's own part, and a result with a number.
7. **Paragraph 3 — a second piece of evidence or a differentiator** (research, industry, cross-disciplinary background)
   and why it matters for this role.
8. **Paragraph 4 — practicalities and close**: language levels; earliest start or notice period (Kündigungsfrist);
   for non-EU citizens the residence / work-permit status (e.g. Blue Card); willingness to talk in person.
   **No salary** unless the posting explicitly asks for a Gehaltsvorstellung — then ask the user for the figure.
9. **Sign-off**: `Mit freundlichen Grüßen` / `Kind regards` + name.

## Style
10. Do not retell the CV. Explain the why and the how behind it.
11. Every sentence carries content. No filler words (passionate, thrilled, excited, delve, leverage, synergy, dynamic,
    "team player"), few dashes, no triple parallel phrases.

## Self-check
12. Dates, durations and numbers match the CV exactly (CV says 2 years 3 months → the letter may not say "two and a half years").
13. The posting's 3–5 most important keywords appear in both the CV and the letter.
14. The company fact in paragraph 1 is verifiable; keep its source URL in the application folder.
